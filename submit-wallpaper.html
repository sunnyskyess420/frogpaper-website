The Process

How submission works

01

Submit the form

Attach your wallpaper, add the details, and hit submit. You'll keep your place on the page the whole time.

02

Review

Submissions are reviewed for quality, originality, and appropriateness. This typically takes 1–3 business days.

03

Approval & publication

Approved wallpapers are added to the community gallery slideshow on the main website, credited to you.

Keep in Mind

Submission guidelines

  • Wallpapers must be AI-generated using FrogPaper or similar AI tools.
  • Accepted resolutions: 1920×1080 (Landscape), 1080×1920 (Portrait), or 1080×1080 (Square).
  • File size: maximum 5 MB. Formats: PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP.
  • Content must be appropriate for all audiences.
  • You must have the rights to share the image — original work or AI-generated with your own prompt.
