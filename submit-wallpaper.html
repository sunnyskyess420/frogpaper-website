How Submission Works

1

Submit Your Wallpaper

Fill out the form below with your wallpaper image, details, and description. Your submission will be sent for review.

2

Review Process

Submissions are reviewed for quality, originality, and appropriateness. This typically takes 1-3 business days.

3

Approval & Publication

Approved wallpapers are added to the community gallery slideshow on the main website.

Submission Guidelines

  • Wallpapers must be AI-generated using FrogPaper or similar AI tools
  • Accepted resolutions: 1920x1080 (Landscape), 1080x1920 (Portrait), or 1080x1080 (Square)
  • File size: Maximum 5MB
  • Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP
  • Content must be appropriate for all audiences
  • You must have rights to share the image (original work or AI-generated with your prompt)
