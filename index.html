Everything in One Window

A complete wallpaper creation and management studio packed into a single desktop app. No subscriptions, no browser tabs.

AI Image Generation

Connects to Pollinations.ai (free, no key), Cloudflare Workers AI, and HuggingFace with FLUX, SDXL, and more models. Start generating immediately.

Structured Prompt Builder

6 dropdown categories (Subject, Mode, Lighting, Color, Setting, Atmosphere) + negative prompt presets + recipe library. Build rich prompts without typing.

Gallery & Curation

7 views with favorites, tagging, sorting, and filtering. Browse, organize, and manage your entire wallpaper collection from one panel.

19 Style Filters

Transform any wallpaper with local artistic filters: Oil Painting, Cyberpunk Neon, Gouache, Vaporwave, and more. All processed on your machine, no cloud cost.

Portrait Bulk Export

New

Bulk export portrait (9:16) images to SD cards, USB drives, or any folder. Get your wallpapers onto your phone in one click.

Smart Slideshow

Auto-rotate wallpapers on a timer (1-60 min) with fullscreen-pause detection and duplicate skipping. Double-click any thumbnail to set instantly.

Fully Private & Local

All images stay on your machine. Only text prompts leave your PC. No images, filenames, or personal data ever uploaded. Open source and auditable.

How It Works

Three steps from idea to desktop. No accounts, no API keys required for the free tier.

01

Build Your Prompt

Use 6 structured dropdowns (Subject, Mode, Lighting, Color, Setting, Atmosphere) to craft a rich prompt. Pick from 100+ subjects, 10 modes, and dozens of lighting/color options. Save your favorites as recipes for one-click reuse.

02

Generate & Style

Hit Generate and watch your wallpaper come to life via FLUX, SDXL, or other models. Apply one of 19 local artistic filters (Oil Painting, Cyberpunk Neon, Gouache, etc.) or add text overlays. All styling happens on your machine.

03

Set, Export & Rotate

Set any wallpaper with a double-click. Let the smart slideshow rotate them automatically (1-60 min, pauses in fullscreen). Bulk export portrait versions to your phone. Your collection, your rules.
