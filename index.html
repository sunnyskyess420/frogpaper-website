AI Wallpaper Studio for Windows

FrogPaper is a cozy desktop studio that combines prompt building, AI image generation, style filtering, and automatic wallpaper rotation — all in one window. No browser. No server. Just your imagination.

Everything You Need

A complete wallpaper creation and management studio packed into a single desktop app.

AI Image Generation

Connects to Pollinations.ai, Cloudflare Workers AI, and HuggingFace with multiple models including FLUX, SDXL, and more — including a fully free, no-key-required option.

Structured Prompt Builder

Build rich prompts with 6 dropdown categories (Subject, Mode, Lighting, Color, Setting, Atmosphere), negative prompt presets, and a recipe library to save and reuse your favorites.

Gallery & Curation

7 gallery views with favorites, tagging, sorting, and filtering. Browse, organize, and manage your entire wallpaper collection from one panel.

19 Style Filters

Transform any wallpaper with local artistic filters — Oil Painting, Cyberpunk Neon, Gouache, Vaporwave, and more. All processed on your machine, no cloud needed.

Auto-Rotation Slideshow

Set a timer (1–60 min) and let your wallpapers rotate automatically. Smart features include fullscreen-pause detection and duplicate skipping.

Fully Private & Local

All images stay on your machine. Only text prompts are sent to your chosen AI provider. No images, filenames, or personal data ever leave your PC.

How It Works

01

Build Your Prompt

Use the sidebar dropdowns to dial in your perfect wallpaper — pick a subject, artistic mode, lighting, colors, setting, and atmosphere. Or load a saved recipe from your library.

02

Generate & Curate

Hit Generate and watch your wallpaper come to life. Apply style filters, add text overlays, tag favorites, and build your personal collection across 7 gallery views.

03

Set & Forget

Apply any image as your Windows wallpaper with one click, or let the slideshow rotate through your collection automatically. It even pauses when you’re in a fullscreen app.
