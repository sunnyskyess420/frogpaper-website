Cloud Sync & More

Your wallpapers follow you everywhere. Save them to the cloud and access them from any device, automatically.

Connect Your Cloud Storage

Link your Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox account and FrogPaper will sync your wallpaper collection there automatically.

Syncs as You Go

New wallpapers are uploaded to your cloud storage the moment they're created. No manual saving or dragging files around.

Secure Sign-In

Cloud connections use standard OAuth sign-in, the same secure method used by apps like Gmail and Spotify. Your credentials stay on your computer.

Smart File Syncing

Only changed files are uploaded, so syncing is fast and doesn't waste bandwidth. If the same file is edited on two devices, FrogPaper handles it gracefully.

Easy Setup Wizard

A simple step-by-step guide walks you through connecting your cloud account. No tech skills needed, just follow the prompts.

25+ Style Filters

Give your wallpapers a unique look with one-click style filters like Vintage, Oil Paint, Cyberpunk, Watercolor, and many more.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how FrogPaper handles your data.

Data Collection

FrogPaper is a desktop application that runs locally on your computer. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or generated images to external servers.

Cloud Storage Credentials

When you connect cloud storage providers (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox), your OAuth tokens are stored locally on your machine using secure credential management. These tokens are never shared with third parties or transmitted to our servers.

AI Image Generation

Image generation requests are sent directly to AI service providers (Pollinations, FLUX) based on your configuration. These providers may have their own privacy policies, but FrogPaper does not store or track your prompts or generated images.

Telemetry

FrogPaper does not include any telemetry, analytics, or usage tracking. The application is completely offline-capable and does not require an internet connection for core functionality.

Open Source

FrogPaper is open source software. You can review the complete source code on GitHub to verify how the application handles your data. We welcome security audits and contributions from the community.

Updates

The built-in update checker only queries GitHub's public API to check for new releases. No personal information is transmitted during update checks.
