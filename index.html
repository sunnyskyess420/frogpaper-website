Two apps, one idea

FrogPaper is really
two different apps.

A full desktop studio for Windows, and a phone-first studio for Android. They share the same idea — words in, wallpapers out — but each one stands on its own. Here is what each app actually does.

Windows · desktop

FrogPaper for Windows

The full studio for your PC — generate, style, sort and sync a whole wallpaper library.

  • • Generate from 6 AI providers — Pollinations, Cloudflare, HuggingFace, Prodia, Replicate, Fal.ai
  • • 25+ style filters, with control over size and seed
  • • Sets and rotates your Windows wallpaper; browse it as a gallery
  • • Cloud sync (Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox) and the Photo Sorter companion
  • • Full keyboard control

Android · mobile

FrogPaper Mobile

A phone-first studio — generate from a few words and keep every wallpaper on your own device.

  • • Describe a wallpaper in a few words, or roll the dice for a surprise
  • • Build screen: subject, style, lighting, colour, mood, atmosphere
  • • Gallery on your phone — star favourites, search by prompt, sort & filter

  • • Rotate automatically (every open or once a day); set the home or lock screen
  • • Works offline from your copies; free engine by default, or bring your own key
  • • No account, nothing uploaded anywhere

Features

Six AI providers, one app.

Pick the backend that fits your budget and speed — free options, pay-per-image, or premium quality. Switch anytime with dynamic setup guides.

6 AI Providers

Pollinations (free), Cloudflare Workers AI (free), HuggingFace, Prodia, Replicate, and Fal.ai. Switch in Settings and the UI adapts instantly.

Dynamic Provider Setup

Switch providers and the panel shows only that provider's API key field plus a step-by-step setup guide with clickable links. No guesswork.

Resolution Freedom

Choose 16:9, portrait, or square. If a provider only outputs 1024×1024, FrogPaper resizes to your target with lossless LANCZOS scaling.

Blazing Fast Polling

Replicate generations dropped from ~65s to ~8s thanks to optimized 1-second polling instead of slow long-poll connections.

Star-Pinned Favorites

Every dropdown option carries its own star button. Pin your go-to subjects, moods, and lighting — they rise to the top, always one click away.

Safer by Design

API keys are never packed into the EXE. Fresh installs start from a clean template, and every build passes an automated secret scan before packaging.

Release Notes

What's new in v1.6.0

The visual refresh: harmonized colours, surfaces, spacing, and type across all 18 themes — plus scrolling that finally works in every window, a launch that no longer flashes, and frogs that only visit when invited.

Visual Refresh

  • New palette tokens — elevated surfaces, muted text, stronger hairlines — extended to every one of the 18 dark and light themes.
  • Buttons, tabs, and cards now share one calm surface hierarchy.
  • Spacing and type polish across the whole app.

Scrolling, everywhere

  • Mouse-wheel scrolling now works in the Settings window, tutorial windows, and dialogs — not just the gallery and sidebar.
  • Wheel events route to the page you are actually hovering, so nothing scrolls behind your back.
  • Pinned dropdowns keep their own scrolling, and popups stay open while you navigate with the keyboard.

Smoother first impressions

  • No more theme flash on launch — the window appears finished, already in your theme.
  • The window builds and themes while hidden, then shows itself ready.

Under the Hood

  • 304 automated tests — including new wheel-scroll regression tests for the gallery, settings, and dialogs.
  • Dependency security bumps (nltk, pillow, opencv-python, sentence-transformers, huggingface-hub).
  • The test suite no longer touches your real config.json.

Frog Sneak

  • The Frog Dial got manners — a new dropdown in Settings > Generation controls how often a frog sneaks into blank-subject random runs.
  • Pick your odds: off, rare (~5%), classic (~12%), or party (~50%). Default is classic.
  • No more hard-coded frog bias; daily runs vary it on their own.

Upgrading to 1.6.0

Short version: run the new installer over the old one — your stuff stays.

Will I lose my wallpapers, favorites, or settings? +

No. Upgrading never touches your data: wallpapers, the gallery with its tags, pinned favorites, presets, your config, and cloud connections all carry over untouched.

How do I upgrade? +

Download the 1.6.0 installer and run it over your existing install. That's it. From source, extract the update zip over your project folder and allow replacing all files.

Do I need to set up my AI provider again? +

No. Your provider choice, API keys, and settings load exactly as they were.

What should I try first after updating? +

Open Settings and scroll any page — the wheel finally works there. Then relaunch: the window appears already dressed in your theme.

Does 1.6.0 look different? +

Softly, yes — the Visual Refresh harmonizes colours, surfaces, spacing, and type across all 18 themes. Nothing moves; it just feels more finished.
